Ghostwire sends text between phones as high-pitched sound. No Wi-Fi, no Bluetooth, just speaker to mic.
Open this page on two phones, tap Power on on both, pick the same band, then type.

Phones about 1 m apart, volume up, silent mode off. Dial-up band is audible and more reliable if ultrasonic struggles.
